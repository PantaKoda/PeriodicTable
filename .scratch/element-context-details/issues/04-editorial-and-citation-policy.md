# Set the editorial and citation policy

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02

## Question

What claim length, reading level, terminology rules, source display, uncertainty language, and fallback wording should every element-context record follow across English, Swedish, and Greek?

## Comments

## Answer

Each field is one or two plain-language sentences and must preserve material qualifiers such as isotope, compound, trace production, and scientific uncertainty. “Natural occurrence” describes reservoirs or production pathways; “free form” answers the narrower native/uncombined question and never treats occurrence in a compound as native occurrence. Biological copy states essentiality, presence without a known role, no known role, or relevant toxicity without giving exposure or medical advice. Uses distinguish established, specialized, research-only, and no known practical use.

Every field carries at least one approved source ID. The drawer shows the record’s unique source links and review date. English is the semantic master; Swedish and Greek may use natural syntax but cannot add, omit, or strengthen claims. Missing evidence must be written as an explicit unknown, never hidden by empty content or fallback language.
