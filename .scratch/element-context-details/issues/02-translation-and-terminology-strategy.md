# Choose the translation and terminology strategy

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

What authoritative terminology references and review workflow should govern English, Swedish, and Greek element-context prose so that all three languages remain scientifically accurate, natural to read, and equivalent in meaning?

The answer must address localized chemistry vocabulary, names of minerals/compounds and biological concepts, source-language translation, terminology consistency, and a validation approach feasible for 118 elements.

## Comments

## Answer

Use a source-backed English record as the concept-locked semantic master, then translate Swedish and Greek independently from that master. All languages share claim IDs, statuses, uncertainty, and source IDs while allowing natural target-language syntax. Maintain a versioned multilingual glossary, prohibit silent English fallback, and require bilingual revision plus native-language review; chemistry sign-off is mandatory for biological, radioactive, medical, disputed-occurrence, and superheavy-element claims.

The complete authority hierarchy, controlled UI terminology, translation rules, twelve-batch workflow, and automated release gates are recorded in [Translation and terminology strategy](../research/translation-and-terminology-strategy.md).
